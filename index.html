About

Hello, I’m Elena.

I’m a photographer based in Lincoln, Nebraska, and I believe the Midwest has the best light in America. Low, golden, and honest — it asks nothing of you except that you show up as yourself.

For eight years I’ve photographed weddings in barns and backyards, portraits in wheat fields, and editorial stories for brands that want to feel like somewhere real. My sessions are unhurried and unposed: I’ll give you direction when you need it and disappear when you don’t.

When I’m not shooting, I’m printing in my home darkroom or walking the trails at Pioneers Park with my dog, Biscuit.
